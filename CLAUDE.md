# Project rules — Hemp Bev Co redesign

- The website must feel smooth and cinematic everywhere: eased scroll-driven motion (lerped, ~0.075 smoothing), "pour" easing cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), staggered reveals, no janky jumps or abrupt cuts. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Hero product carousels show ONLY the current product — never show blurred/faded previous or next products.
- Typography: Bebas Neue (display) + Manrope (body). Colors from the logo (copper #C8935A, olive #6F7D2B, sage #CDD7B1, cream #FAF6EE, ink #23261A).
- Product images: use transparent cutouts in assets/cutout/ on colored/dark backgrounds.
